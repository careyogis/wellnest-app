Elders E-Choupal

The prevalence of loneliness among elderly in India is significantly high.Those with fewer social networks and less participation in social activities have higher odds of loneliness.

We at WellnesT through our experience have found a unique solution- CareYogi e-Choupal. It is a online community of elders who can remain anonymous but share their hearts out. Connecting with likeminded individuals of their age gives them the Experience, Strength and Hope of others to recover from their feeling of loneliness.
