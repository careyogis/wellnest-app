About Our Emergency Alert & Response Services

Because emergencies don’t come announced.

Emergency Alert & Response is CAREYOGI’s tech-enabled emergency response ecosystem for seniors living at home.

It provides:

  • Emergency Alert Device– One-touch alert to call for help instantly
  • 24/7 Emergency Response – Our team is always on standby
  • Ambulance Services– Quick coordination for urgent transfers
  • On-Demand Nursing Visits – Skilled nurses dispatched as needed
  • Live Tracking via CAREYOGI App– Families can monitor response in real time
