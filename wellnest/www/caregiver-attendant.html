About Our Attendant Care Program

Because dignity is built on daily comfort.
For many elders, simple daily tasks become difficult with age. Attendant Care ensures Assistance with Daily Living so seniors can enjoy life without hesitation, without burdening anyone’s routine.

Services include:

  • Help with bathing, grooming, brushing, and shaving
  • Mobility support– safe assistance while moving indoors or outdoors
  • Meals and feeding assistance – ensuring nutrition and dignity
  • Companionship – someone to listen, walk, or accompany on visits
  • Outings and occasions – a trusted companion for park strolls, family events, or social visits
