What You Get

01. DrRing Emergency Device

Wearable emergency device with a simple one-touch SOS button. Wear as a pendant or on your wrist for immediate call for help.

02. Condition-Based Emergency Kit

Personalized emergency kit tailored to patient health conditions, containing essential medicines and critical emergency supplies.

03. Specialist Consultation

Includes one complimentary specialist consultation to assess health condition and build personalized emergency preparedness needs.

04. Emergency Doctor Support

Access specialized emergency doctors over phone or video during a medical crisis for step-by-step guidance.

How Emergency Assist Works

  1. Press for Help: Patient presses DrRing wearable button or SOS in CareYogi app.
  2. Emergency Alert Initiated: Request carries vital info and triggers emergency response.
  3. Family Gets Alert: Designated family members view patient status & location in real time.
  4. Escalate When Required: Escalates to CareYogi medical responder network if family unavailable.
  5. Emergency Assistance: Matched with available, verified medical responder for remote or physical support.
  6. Incident Closure & Follow-up: Digital incident log retained for medical continuity and hospital care.

Designed Especially for Families Caring from a Distance

  • Senior citizens living alone or at home during night hours
  • Elderly parents whose children live in another city or country (NRIs)
  • Patients with chronic conditions at risk of sudden medical emergencies
Disclaimer: Emergency Assist provides technology-enabled emergency communication, care coordination, and access to available medical responders. It does not replace local emergency medical services or hospital emergency care. In life-threatening emergencies, contact local emergency services immediately.
