Medicines at Home — Coordinated Doorstep Delivery

Get your prescribed medicines coordinated and delivered right to your doorstep. CareYogi makes it easier to continue your treatment at home by seamlessly connecting your doctor's prescriptions with trusted local pharmacy partners.

Your prescription, medicines, and order history remain connected to your CareYogi Health Vault, making ongoing treatment easier for you and your family to manage remotely or in person.

Key Features & Services

Prescription-Based Ordering

Order medicines directly from prescriptions stored in your CareYogi Health Vault without re-uploading.

Smart Rx Information

Converts digital prescriptions into easy-to-understand medication lists showing dosage, frequency, and duration at a glance.

Medicine Refill Support

Easily initiate refill requests for chronic conditions before running out of essential long-term medications.

Remote Family Management

Authorized family members or NRIs can place and track medicine orders for elderly parents from anywhere.

How It Works

Doctor prescribes → Prescription added to CareYogi → Smart Rx extracts medicines → Select items to order → CareYogi coordinates with local pharmacy partner → Medicines delivered to home → Order history updated in Health Vault

Who Can Benefit?

  • Senior citizens & patients with chronic conditions taking multiple daily medications
  • Post-surgical patients requiring uninterrupted long-term treatment
  • NRIs and adult children managing medicine refills for elderly parents living alone
