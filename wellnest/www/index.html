Care Economy

Destigmatise Professional Elder Care: Professional parental care is much more seamless, organised and structured than what ones loved ones can offer. According to 'The Caring Company Report' by Harvard Business School, American companies are facing a caregiving crisis. Changing demographics over the past few decades have put great pressure on American employees as they try to balance work and care responsibilities. Yet many employers remain largely oblivious to the growing costs of this hidden “care economy”— costs that hurt employers and employees alike. Similar is the case in India. It is time that human productivity is maximised by letting professionals do what family need not .
